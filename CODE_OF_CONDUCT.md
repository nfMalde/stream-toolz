# Code of conduct

Stream Toolz is made by a streamer, for streamers. Everyone is welcome here, whatever their channel size, experience
or background.

- **Be kind.** Disagree with ideas, never attack people. No insults, harassment, discrimination or sexual content.
- **Be constructive.** Explain what you mean and why; assume good intentions; help newcomers find their way.
- **Respect privacy.** Do not post other people's personal data, and no chat messages or viewer names from your
  stream without their consent. The app's logs hold none, keep it that way in issues too.
- **Keep it on topic.** Issues and pull requests are about Stream Toolz. Other tools are neither named nor judged
  here; say what Stream Toolz does and what it does not do.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close issues and block people who do not keep to this. If something
bothers you, write to the maintainer through a [private security report](https://github.com/nfMalde/stream-toolz/security/advisories/new)
or a direct message on one of the channels in the README; it stays confidential.
